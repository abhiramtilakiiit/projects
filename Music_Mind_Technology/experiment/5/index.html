An old man, exhausted and left behind in the chaos of war, sits by a bridge as people flee. An observer, whose job is to scout the enemy's advance, encounters him. The old man explains he was the last to leave his town, San Carlos, because he was taking care of his animals: two goats, a cat, and four pairs of pigeons. He worries deeply about their fate, especially the goats and pigeons, now that he has been forced to leave due to the approaching artillery. Despite being urged to move on towards safety, the old man is too tired and preoccupied with the well-being of his abandoned animals.
